File format

Two columns: the tutor, then their Activation Director. A header row is detected automatically; without one, columns are read in that order. The tutor is matched on email, phone or DB ID — whichever you have. The Director is matched on their work email, and must already be an active team member. Rows that match nothing are skipped and listed back to you, so a partial file is safe to upload.
